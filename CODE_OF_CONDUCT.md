# Community code of conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

We welcome contributors and users of all backgrounds. Be respectful, explain
disagreements constructively, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing,
and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Never share
someone else's documents, credentials, or private diagnostics without permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, restrict access,
or ban participants. Enforcement should be proportional, consistent, and based on
the behavior. Maintainers are also bound by these rules.

Flag inappropriate public content using GitHub's reporting/moderation controls or
mention the maintainer (@daozen) without repeating personal or sensitive details.
For harassment or threats requiring a private report, use GitHub's "Report abuse"
channel. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project-maintained community spaces. It does not promise moderation of independent
forks or external services.
