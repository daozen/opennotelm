# Learning through small experiments

## Practice

Small improvements accumulate over time. Careful practice and patience support
sustained learning. A useful experiment has one clear question and an observable result.

## Reflection

After each experiment, write down what changed and what remains uncertain.
Review the original evidence before turning an observation into a conclusion.

## Sharing

Explain the idea with a concrete example. Keep quotations linked to their source.
Distinguish an observed result from a helpful analogy.
